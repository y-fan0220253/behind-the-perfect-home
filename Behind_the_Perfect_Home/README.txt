BEHIND THE PERFECT HOME — FMP PROGRESS WEBSITE

OPENING
- Open index.html in Chrome or Safari (files stay inside the assets folder).
- Or open the standalone HTML supplied separately; it embeds all 5 collage images.

DEMONSTRATION
1. Entrance: click the apartment door.
2. Living room: click the calendar or any of the other five active objects.
3. Use the left/right arrows to browse reconstructed research case cards.
4. Tap the heart, bookmark, or speech bubble; add a temporary reader reflection.
5. In the living room, click Kitchen, Balcony, Bedroom. From each room, use Back to the living room.
6. Open Research status at the top-right for the data scope and research limitations.

CONTENT STATUS
- The existing 24 selected Rednote post cases (P001-P024) are incorporated as editorial, English-language case summaries.
- Two explicitly defined sample-wide counts are shown where relevant: 3/24 posts with documented planning artifacts (P001 P018 P019), and 3/24 foregrounding men visibly performing housework (P020 P022 P024).
- Other numbers such as 3 CASES or 4 CASES refer only to the examples shown in that interaction, not entire-platform statistics.
- Original comment quotations, original post engagement and gendered comment comparison remain blank (—) pending systematic data collection. All visible user interactions are local to this page and are not source data.
- Case imagery is reconstructed editorial presentation, NOT original user screenshots / real account identities.
